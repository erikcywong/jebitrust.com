Everybody in the chain — the drinker, the franchisee, the lender, the investor — is asked to trust a stranger's claim. JEBI TRUST converts those claims into auditable evidence, binds the evidence to three codes, and charges for access to the evidence instead of taking a cut of the coffee. 链条上的每个人——消费者、加盟商、放款人、投资者——都被要求相信一个陌生人的说法。JEBI TRUST 把这些说法变成可审计的证据，把证据绑定到三个码上，并对“调用证据”收费，而不是从咖啡里抽成。

01 / The problem问题

Silence is priced as guilt沉默会被定价为有罪

In coffee, the information gap is not only about quality — it is about existence. Does the farm exist, is the receipt real, was the carbon actually removed, will the franchisor still answer the phone in year three? Four buyers, four identical defaults. 咖啡里的信息不对称不只是品质，而是“是否存在”。农场真的存在吗？仓单是真的吗？碳真的被移除过吗？三年后加盟总部还会接电话吗？四类买方，四种一模一样的默认判断。

BUYER 01

The drinker消费者

"Unknown roaster = bad cup." Pays a premium only for names already in their head. A better origin cannot enter on merit. “不认识的烘焙商＝难喝。”只为脑子里已有的名字付溢价。更好的产区无法凭品质进场。

price penalty
BUYER 02

The franchisee加盟商

"Unknown franchisor = my deposit is gone." Demands a buy-back or walks. Good operators stay small. “不认识的总部＝押金打水漂。”要么要求回购，要么直接走。好品牌长不大。

growth ceiling
BUYER 03

The lender放款人

"Unknown receipt = fake collateral." Discounts to salvage value, or declines. Post-harvest finance stays expensive. “不认识的仓单＝假抵押。”按残值打折，或者直接拒贷。采收后融资一直很贵。

collateral haircut
BUYER 04

The investor投资者

"Unverified asset = bad asset." Prices every real asset at the worst case. Then calls coffee uninvestable. “未确权资产＝烂资产。”把所有真实资产按最坏情况定价，然后说咖啡不可投。

junk pricing

Each of these is a pricing penalty, not a moral problem — and every penalty is payable to whoever removes it. That is the business. 这四项都不是道德问题，而是定价惩罚——而每一笔惩罚，都会付给把它消除掉的人。这就是生意。

02 / The product产品

Trust, sold by the verified event按“已验证事件”出售信任

We do not sell certainty. Certainty is not for sale. We sell checkable claims. 我们不卖“确定”。确定是买不到的。我们卖的是可核查的说法。

The unit is the Verified Event (VE): any assertion that changes someone's willingness to pay, lend or invest — carrying an evidence hash, a timestamp, an accountable owner, and a code you can look up. 计价单位是已验证事件（Verified Event, VE）：任何会改变某人支付、放款或投资意愿的断言，携带证据哈希、时间戳、责任主体，以及一个可查询的编号。

A café that cannot show its monthly VE count is a café nobody can underwrite. That is the sales argument, and it does not require anyone to like us. 一家说不出自己每月有多少 VE 的咖啡馆，就是一家没人敢承保的咖啡馆。这就是销售逻辑，而且不需要对方喜欢我们。

CODE 01 / BATCH

Batch code批次码

Pod → green bean → roast → pack → shelf → store. One unbroken lineage per lot, with yield, cup score and sensor trace attached to it. 种植舱 → 生豆 → 烘焙 → 包装 → 货架 → 门店。每一批一条不断裂的血缘，附带产量、杯测分与传感器曲线。

CODE 02 / ASSET

Asset code资产码

Any thing that can be owned and financed: a pod, a warehouse receipt, a tonne of removed carbon, a recipe, a store's cash flow, a REIT unit. 任何可被拥有与融资的标的：舱体、仓单、一吨已移除的碳、一个配方、一家门店的现金流、一份 REITs 份额。

CODE 03 / TOKEN

Token code通证码

A claim on an asset code. It cannot exist on its own: no token without an asset, no asset without batch lineage, no listing until all nine checks pass. 对某个资产码的索取权。它不能独立存在：无资产不发通证，无批次血缘不算资产，九项检验未全部通过不得上链。

03 / Revenue收入

Six lines, none of them a coffee margin六条收入线，没有一条来自咖啡差价

Metered, contracted, and priced off the cost of producing the evidence — never off the size of the asset or the return it might produce. 按量计量、以合同锁定，定价锚定“生产证据的成本”，绝不锚定资产规模或它可能产生的回报。

04 / Refusals拒绝清单

A trust business is defined by the money it turns down信任生意的定义，是它拒绝收的钱

Every trust franchise in history died the same way: it sold the thing it was supposed to certify. These four refusals are written into the group's constitutional documents, not into a policy PDF. 历史上每一个信任机构都以同一种方式死掉：把它本该认证的东西卖掉了。以下四条拒绝，写进集团章程文件，而不是写成一份政策 PDF。

R1
No success fees on capital raised不收取融资成功费We are never paid more because more money moved. Placement economics create the incentive to over-issue.募到的钱越多，我们不会赚得越多。承销经济会直接制造超发的动机。
R2
No issuer may own its custodian or its rater发行方不得拥有托管方或评级方Structural separation, not a conflict-of-interest policy.结构性隔离，不是一份利益冲突政策。
R3
No guaranteed anything不做任何“保证”No principal protection, no fixed yield, no "zero risk", no "absolute carbon neutral". A single such sentence ends the licence and the franchise.不保本、不保固定收益、不说“零风险”、不说“绝对零碳”。一句话就能同时终结牌照与品牌。
R4
No selling access to investors不卖“接触投资者”的机会We price evidence. Distribution is licensed, limited, and never auctioned to the highest issuer.我们只对证据定价。分销须持牌、受限，且绝不向出价最高的发行方拍卖。
05 / Admission准入

Nine checks. All of them. Or no token.九项检验，全部通过，否则不发通证

No score, no rating, no "partially ready". Each check is a document that either exists or does not, issued by someone who is not the issuer. A single failure means the asset is unlistable — and saying so is the product. 不打分、不评级、没有“基本就绪”。每一项检验就是一份文件：存在，或不存在；由非发行方出具。一项失败即不可挂牌——而敢说出来，才是产品本身。

01 · Title权属

Ownership documents, registration and unbroken batch lineage.权属凭证、登记记录、不断裂的批次血缘。

02 · Custody独立托管

Licensed third party, segregated account, monthly reconciliation.持牌第三方、独立账户、月度对账。

03 · Insurance保险

Policy naming the asset, loss payee is the SPV.保单载明标的，赔款受益人为 SPV。

04 · Inspection质检核查

Independent sampling, with re-inspection built in.独立抽样，并内置复检机制。

05 · Legal opinion法律定性

Written view from licensed counsel, plus licence or exemption.持牌律师书面意见，附牌照或豁免。

06 · Segregated cash资金隔离

Bank account under trustee control, waterfall published.受托人控制下的银行账户，清偿顺序已公布。

07 · Audit审计

External opinion on the vehicle, engaged by the vehicle.针对该载体的外部审计，由载体自行聘请。

08 · Disclosure披露机制

Quarterly report, 24-hour incident notice, version log.季度报告、24 小时事件公告、版本记录。

09 · Wind-down处置预案

Disposal path and waterfall agreed before subscription.认购前已约定处置路径与清偿顺序。

One failed check = no listing, no investor materials, no whitelist entry. There is no conditional pass and no "to be provided later". 一项失败＝不挂牌、不出投资者材料、不进白名单。没有附条件通过，也没有“后续补交”。

07 / Sequencing排序

Not all coffee assets deserve a token不是所有咖啡资产都配得上一枚通证

Assets are ranked by verifiability per unit of legal risk — not by size, story, or how badly the owner needs cash. 排序依据是“每单位法律风险对应的可验证性”，不是规模、不是故事，也不是资产方有多缺钱。
