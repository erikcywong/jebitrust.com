04 / Assets资产

Not every coffee asset deserves a token不是每一项咖啡资产都配得上一枚通证

The sequence is set by one ratio: how much of the asset can be proven, divided by how much legal risk the container adds. Receipts score well. Store cash flow does not — not because the coffee is worse, but because the wrapper is heavier than the thing inside it. 排序只由一个比值决定：这项资产有多少可被证明，除以这个“容器”带来多少法律风险。仓单得分高，门店现金流不高——不是因为咖啡更差，而是因为外面的壳比里面的东西还重。

4.1 Ordering principle排序原则

Verifiability first, yield story last先看可验证性，最后才看收益故事

Every asset class is asked the same six questions before it is allowed anywhere near a token: 每一类资产在接近通证之前，都要回答同样的六个问题：

  • Can title be proven by a document a third party issued?权属能否由第三方签发的文件证明？
  • Can the asset be held by someone who is not the issuer?资产能否由非发行方持有？
  • Does the cash arrive in an account the issuer cannot touch?现金是否进入发行方无法动用的账户？
  • Is the legal character settled in writing, or assumed?法律定性是书面确定的，还是假设的？
  • Would a decline in this asset be visible before it becomes a loss?这项资产的恶化，是否在变成损失之前就能被看见？
  • If the group disappeared tomorrow, could a stranger take possession?如果集团明天消失，陌生人能否取得占有？

Six yeses move an asset up the ladder. Any "we'll sort that out later" moves it down, regardless of size. 六个“是”让资产上移；任何一句“这个以后再说”让它下移，无论规模多大。

4.2 The six classes六个类别

Structure, legal question, admission condition, and the way it usually breaks结构、法律问题、准入条件，以及它通常怎么坏

01 · FIRST第一优先

Warehouse receipt咖啡仓单

The only coffee asset that is already a financial instrument in most producing jurisdictions. Start here because nothing has to be invented. 在多数产区，这是唯一已经是金融工具的咖啡资产。从它开始，因为不需要发明任何东西。

Structure结构SPV holds title → licensed warehouse custodies → bank receives pledge → registry writes the lock before funds release.SPV 持所有权 → 持牌仓库托管 → 银行受押 → 放款前先在登记处写入锁定。
Legal Q法律问题Document of title, or contractual claim? Does a transferable token constitute a security?它是所有权凭证，还是合同请求权？可流转的通证是否构成证券？
Admission准入9/9 + bank pledge agreement. A transferable token additionally needs the opinion to permit it.银行质押协议。可流转通证还须法律意见允许。
Breaks via失效方式Double pledge · phantom stock · quality drift at re-inspection.重复质押 · 空单 · 复检品质漂移。
registry lock登记锁定independent sampler独立抽样shrinkage insurance短量险
02

Carbon removal碳移除量

Verified removals are serialised, retireable and finite — which makes them the cleanest digital asset in the chain, and the easiest to fake at the edges. 经核查的移除量可序列化、可注销、总量有限——是链上最干净的数字资产，也最容易在边缘作假。

Structure结构Per-batch MRV → serial numbers → registry retirement account held by the SPV, not the seller.按批次 MRV → 序列号 → 由 SPV（而非卖方）持有的登记处注销账户。
Legal Q法律问题Environmental attribute or financial product? Tax and cross-border treatment?环境属性还是金融产品？税务与跨境如何处理？
Admission准入9/9 + independent methodology review and a funded buffer pool.独立方法学核查 + 已注入缓冲池。
Breaks via失效方式Double counting · reversal (permanence) · vintage fraud.重复计算 · 逆转（持久性） · 年份造假。
serial retirement序列注销buffer pool缓冲池no forward sale不做远期销售
03

Grow pod & store equipment种植舱与门店设备

Aeroponic pods are high-value, serialised, telemetric and movable — the best and worst property of equipment as collateral is that it can be relocated. 雾化舱单价高、有序号、可遥测、可搬动——设备作为抵押品最好也最坏的一点，就是它能被搬走。

Structure结构SPV owns, operator leases; title registry by serial; telemetry as continuous possession evidence.SPV 持有、运营方承租；按序号登记产权；遥测作为持续占有的证据。
Legal Q法律问题Is perfection of title required by filing? Do fractional lease receivables become securities?产权完善是否需要登记备案？拆分后的租赁应收是否构成证券？
Admission准入9/9 + title registration by serial number and bound telemetry.按序号完成产权登记并绑定遥测。
Breaks via失效方式Unperfected title · equipment relocated · operator abandons the site.产权未完善 · 设备被转移 · 运营方弃场。
serial registry序号登记loss-payee clause保险赔款受益人条款recovery plan per site逐场回收预案
04

Recipe, curve & trademark royalty配方、曲线与商标版税

The most valuable thing a coffee company owns and the least documented. Registration is cheap; royalty collection is not. 咖啡公司最有价值、也最缺文件的东西。登记很便宜，版税归集不便宜。

Structure结构IP holdco → licence agreements → royalty account → per-licensee audit rights.IP 控股 → 许可合同 → 版税账户 → 对被许可方的审计权。
Legal Q法律问题Is a royalty interest a security? Assignment or licence? Moral rights and termination risk?版税收益权是否构成证券？转让还是许可？精神权利与终止风险？
Admission准入9/9 + audited royalty account and an exercised licensee audit right.经审计的版税账户 + 实际行使过被许可方审计权。
Breaks via失效方式Licensee under-reports sales · infringement · key-person dependency.被许可方少报销售 · 侵权 · 关键人依赖。
POS-linked royalty calcPOS 联动版税计算escrowed source源文件托管no projected royalties不预测未来版税
05 · HANDLE WITH COUNSEL须律师介入

Store & mall cash flow门店与商场现金流

In most jurisdictions this is a collective investment, full stop. It is not a payment solution with a blockchain appendix. 在多数辖区，这就是集合投资，没有例外。它不是“支付方案加一个区块链附录”。

Structure结构SPV holds receivables or revenue-share rights; direct POS capture; landlord consent; store-level P&L.SPV 持有应收或收入分成权；POS 直连归集；业主同意；门店级损益。
Legal Q法律问题Security / CIS in almost every case. Which exemption, which investor scope, which disclosure regime?几乎必然构成证券/集合投资计划。适用哪项豁免、哪类投资者、哪套披露制度？
Admission准入9/9 + the relevant licence, obtained before listing.挂牌前已取得相应牌照。
Breaks via失效方式Revenue diversion · related-party leases · cannibalisation between outlets.收入转移 · 关联租赁 · 门店互相蚕食。
no guaranteed rent不承诺保底租金no public solicitation不公开劝诱qualified investors only仅合格投资者
06 · LAST最后

REIT unitsREITs 份额

A REIT is already a regulated product with a trustee, a manager, a valuation cycle and a distribution rule. A token adds a transfer layer — it does not add a shortcut. REITs 本身已是受监管产品，有受托人、管理人、估值周期与分派规则。通证只增加一层流转，不增加任何捷径。

Structure结构REIT holds income property → units registered as before → token used only as a record / transfer instrument where lawful.REIT 持有收益型物业 → 份额照常登记 → 通证仅在合法范围内作为记录/流转工具。
Legal Q法律问题Double regulation. Is the token itself a security? Which venue may list it?双重监管。通证本身是否构成证券？哪个场所可以挂牌？
Admission准入9/9 + licence + full REIT disclosure, with the token as a record layer only.牌照 + 完整 REITs 披露；通证仅作记录层。
Breaks via失效方式Using the token to bypass REIT licensing, investor limits or property valuation rules.用通证规避 REITs 牌照、投资者限制或物业估值规则。
never a wrapper绝不做外壳same disclosure as the REIT与 REITs 同一套披露
4.3 Never永不做

Six things we will not put on a chain六样东西，永不上链

Each of these has been sold somewhere in the world as an "RWA". Each has ended the same way: a token representing a promise that nobody was obligated to keep. 这六样，都曾在世界某处被当作“RWA”卖出去过。结局也都一样：一枚代表“没人有义务兑现的承诺”的通证。

N1
Future franchise fees未来加盟费Not yet earned, not yet contracted, not yet collected. A projection dressed as a receivable.未赚到、未签约、未收到。把预测打扮成应收。
N2
A harvest that has not been grown还没种出来的收成Weather, disease and biology are the counterparties. We do not sell the weather.天气、病害与生物学才是对手方。我们不卖天气。
N3
Platform GMV平台 GMVTurnover is not cash flow, and neither is a claim on a ratio we control the definition of.成交额不是现金流，更不是对“由我们定义口径的比率”的索取权。
N4
Unverified carbon, or a "zero-carbon" label未核查的碳，或“零碳”标签A label is a statement, not an asset. Only retired, serialised, third-party-verified tonnes are assets.标签是陈述，不是资产。只有已注销、有序列号、经第三方核查的吨数才是资产。
N5
A receivable without delivery proof无交付凭证的应收Invoice plus signed delivery plus counterparty confirmation, or it is not a receivable.发票 + 签收 + 对手方确认，缺一不构成应收。
N6
Any asset whose only evidence we wrote ourselves唯一证据由我们自己出具的任何资产Self-attestation is the definition of the problem, not the solution to it.自我出具证明，正是问题的定义，而不是问题的解法。
4.4 Sequencing logic排序逻辑

Each step funds the credibility of the next每一步都为下一步的可信度付费

STEP 01

Receipt仓单

Proves the hardest thing: that we can hold a real asset, with a real custodian, and prevent double pledge.证明最难的事：我们能持有真实资产、真实托管，并阻止重复质押。

  • Bank partner live银行合作落地
  • Registry lock working登记锁定生效
STEP 02

Carbon碳

Proves we can measure and retire a non-physical asset without double counting it.证明我们能计量并注销非物理资产，且不重复计算。

  • Methodology accepted方法学获接受
  • Serial-level retirement序列级注销
STEP 03

Equipment & IP设备与 IP

Proves title perfection and royalty discipline — the two things a coffee operator has never had to do.证明产权完善与版税纪律——咖啡运营方从来不需要做的两件事。

  • Serial registry序号登记
  • Royalty account audited版税账户可审计
STEP 04

Cash flow & REIT现金流与 REITs

Only now does the licensed, restricted, heavily-disclosed end of the ladder become possible.到这一步，阶梯末端那个“持牌、受限、重披露”的世界才成为可能。

  • Licence first, listing second先牌照，后挂牌
  • Qualified investors only仅合格投资者

The trap to avoid: starting at step 4 because it is the most exciting, and back-filling steps 1–3 with documents written after the money arrived. That is how a trust business becomes a story business, and story businesses do not survive a single missed quarter. 要避开的陷阱：因为第 4 步最激动人心就从那里开始，再用“钱到账之后补写的文件”回填第 1–3 步。信任生意就是这样变成故事生意的，而故事生意撑不过一个失约的季度。
