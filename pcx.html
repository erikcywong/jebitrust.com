03 / The index指数

PCX 40 is a floor, not a targetPCX 40 是门槛，不是目标

Provenance Confidence indeX: five dimensions, twenty points each, one number. The number does not say an asset is good. It says whether the evidence around it is complete enough that a stranger could underwrite it — which is the only question that matters before a token exists. 溯源置信指数：五个维度各 20 分，合成一个数字。这个数字不说资产“好”，它说的是——围绕这个资产的证据，是否完整到一个陌生人足以据此承保。这也是通证出现之前唯一重要的问题。

3.1 Why a floor为什么是门槛

A hundred promises cannot be audited. A single threshold can.一百句承诺无法审计，一个阈值可以。

Issuers want gradation because gradation sells. Investors want a line, because a line is falsifiable. PCX keeps both: a continuous score for pricing, and one hard floor at 40 where the answer changes from "yes, with disclosure" to "no". 发行方喜欢渐变，因为渐变好卖；投资者喜欢一条线，因为线可以被证伪。PCX 两者都保留：用连续分数定价，用 40 这一个硬门槛，让答案从“可以，附披露”变成“不行”。

The floor is set where all five dimensions have at least a minimum: a title you can trace, a custodian you did not appoint, cash that arrives in an account you cannot touch, a legal opinion you did not write, and a disclosure that appears whether or not you are having a good quarter. 门槛设在五个维度都达到最低要求的位置：可追溯的权属、非你指定的托管、进入你无法动用的账户的现金、非你撰写的法律意见，以及无论业绩好坏都必须出现的披露。

What PCX is notPCX 不是什么

  • Not a credit rating, unless and until registered under an applicable credit-rating regime.不是信用评级——除非并直到在适用辖区完成信用评级机构注册。
  • Not investment advice, and not a recommendation to buy, hold or sell.不是投资建议，也不构成买入、持有或卖出的推荐。
  • Not a fraud guarantee. A high score means evidence is complete, not that a counterparty is honest.不担保无欺诈。高分意味着证据完整，不意味着对手方诚实。
  • Not comparable across asset classes without the band. 72 on a receipt is not 72 on a store's cash flow.脱离分级不可跨类别比较。仓单的 72 分不等于门店现金流的 72 分。
3.2 Scoring rubric评分细则

Five dimensions, twenty points each五个维度，各 20 分

Each dimension is anchored at 0 / 10 / 20 so two assessors working blind produce the same score within ±4. Inter-assessor variance is published annually. 每个维度设 0 / 10 / 20 三个锚点，使两名独立评估师在盲评下得分差异不超过 ±4 分。评估师间方差每年公开。

3.5 Surveillance持续跟踪

The score has to be able to fall分数必须能够下跌

A rating that only moves upward on request is a certificate of purchase. PCX is rescored whether or not the issuer cooperates, and the most common cause of a downgrade is not fraud — it is a missed disclosure. 只在被要求时才上调的评级，只是一张购买凭证。PCX 无论发行方是否配合都会重新评分；而降级最常见的原因不是欺诈，而是披露逾期。
