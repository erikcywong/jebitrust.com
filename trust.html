JEBI Trust / Trust mechanism信任机制
03 / Admission准入

Nine checks. All of them. Or no token.九项检验，全部通过，否则不发通证。

A score can be negotiated; a checklist cannot. Every check below is binary — the evidence exists or it does not — and no single check can be traded against another. This is deliberately unglamorous: it is the only part of the system an investor can actually verify without trusting us. 分数可以谈，清单不能谈。以下每一项都是“有/无”的判断题——证据存在，或不存在——并且任何一项都不能用另一项的强项来交换。这套机制刻意做得不好看：它是投资者唯一能“不依赖信任我们”就自行核实的部分。

3.1 The nine checks九项检验

Each check names the evidence, and the failure每一项都写明证据是什么，以及什么算失败

Pass means a document exists, is issued by someone other than the issuer, and is current. Anything else is a fail — including "in progress". “通过”意味着：文件存在、由非发行方出具、且在有效期内。其他一切算失败——包括“正在办理”。

01

Title权属

Evidence: ownership or title document, registration record, and unbroken batch lineage. 证据：所有权/权属凭证、登记记录、不断裂的批次血缘。

Fail: internal ledger only, or a claim with no paper behind it.失败：只有内部台账，或背后没有文件的说法。

02

Independent custody独立托管

Evidence: licensed third-party custodian, segregated account, monthly reconciliation statement. 证据：第三方持牌托管、独立账户、月度对账报告。

Fail: the issuer or an affiliate holds the asset.失败：发行方或其关联方自持资产。

03

Insurance保险

Evidence: policy naming the asset, loss payee = the SPV, coverage stated in the disclosure. 证据：保单载明标的、赔款受益人为 SPV、保额写入披露。

Fail: no policy, or the payee is the issuer.失败：无保单，或受益人是发行方。

04

Inspection质检与核查

Evidence: independent sampling report with a re-inspection mechanism and a named sampler. 证据：独立抽样报告，含复检机制与明确的抽样机构。

Fail: self-inspection, or a certificate from a related party.失败：自检，或由关联方出具的证书。

05

Legal opinion法律定性

Evidence: written opinion from licensed counsel, licence or exemption conclusion, defined investor scope. 证据：持牌律师书面意见、牌照或豁免结论、界定的投资者范围。

Fail: an internal view, or an opinion about a different instrument.失败：内部判断，或针对另一种工具的意见。

06

Segregated cash资金隔离

Evidence: collection account at a licensed bank, trustee control, published waterfall. 证据：持牌银行归集账户、受托人控制、已公布的清偿顺序。

Fail: cash commingled with group funds, or no stated priority.失败：资金与集团混同，或未载明优先顺序。

07

Audit审计

Evidence: external audit opinion on the vehicle, auditor engaged by the SPV. 证据：针对该载体的外部审计意见，审计师由 SPV 聘请。

Fail: reliance on group consolidated accounts.失败：依赖集团合并报表代替。

08

Disclosure mechanism披露机制

Evidence: quarterly report, 24-hour incident notice, immutable version log. 证据：季度报告、24 小时事件公告、不可篡改的版本记录。

Fail: annual-only reporting, or disclosure on request.失败：仅年度披露，或“按需提供”。

09

Wind-down plan处置预案

Evidence: disposal path, named liquidator or servicer, priority waterfall published before subscription. 证据：处置路径、明确的清算人或服务商、认购前已公布的清偿顺序。

Fail: "we will deal with it if it happens".失败：“真出事了再说”。

One fail = no listing. No partial pass, no conditional pass, no "will provide on request". An asset that fails any single check cannot be tokenised, cannot appear in investor materials, and cannot enter the whitelist — regardless of size, revenue, partner pressure or how close the deadline is. 一项失败＝不得挂牌。没有“部分通过”、没有“附条件通过”、没有“后续补交”。任何一项不通过的资产，不得发行通证、不得出现在投资者材料、不得进入白名单——不论规模、收入、伙伴压力，或时间节点有多近。

3.2 Access tiers准入分层

What you may see is set by the instrument, not by a number你能看到什么，由工具的法律属性决定，而不是由分数决定

Two assets can both pass all nine checks and still reach completely different audiences, because one is a commodity claim and the other is a security. Treating those two as the same thing is how licences get lost. 两项资产可能都通过九检，却面向完全不同的受众——因为一个是商品性请求权，另一个是证券。把两者当成一回事，就是丢牌照的标准方式。

3.4 After admission准入之后

Admission is a state, not a badge准入是一种状态，不是一枚徽章

A listed asset is re-tested every quarter against the same nine checks. The most common reason an asset leaves the whitelist is not fraud — it is a missed document. That is why removal is automatic and reinstatement is not. 已挂牌资产每季度按同样九项重新检验。资产被移出白名单最常见的原因不是欺诈，而是文件逾期。所以移出是自动的，恢复不是。

T+0
Lapsed evidence removes the asset from the whitelist automatically证据逾期即自动移出白名单System-triggered, not a management decision. Marketing materials must be withdrawn the same day.系统触发，非管理层决定。当日必须撤下相关宣传材料。

Quarterly four-way reconciliation季度四方对账Documents, custody statement, cash report and ledger must agree. Disagreement stops trading, not just reporting.文件、托管对账、资金报告与账本必须一致。不一致时暂停流转，而不只是暂停披露。

3.5 Scope边界

What admission is not准入不是什么

The checks prove that the evidence container is complete. They do not prove the asset is good, the price is fair, or the counterparty is honest. Saying otherwise is how a verification business becomes a liability. 九检证明的是“证据容器完整”，不是资产好、价格公道、对手方诚实。把这三件事说成同一件，就是把核查生意做成负债。

  • Not a credit rating, unless and until registered under an applicable credit-rating regime.不是信用评级——除非并直到在适用辖区完成信用评级机构注册。
  • Not investment advice, and not a recommendation to buy, hold or sell.不是投资建议，也不构成买入、持有或卖出的推荐。
  • Not a fraud guarantee. Complete evidence is not the same as an honest counterparty.不担保无欺诈。证据完整不等于对手方诚实。
  • Not transferable. One asset, one admission. Evidence from a similar asset cannot be reused.不可转让。一资产一准入。相似资产的证据不能复用。

Nine documents is not a high bar to clear — it is a high bar to fake. That is the entire design goal: cheap for an honest operator, expensive for a liar, and visible to anyone who bothers to look. 九份文件，对老实人不算高门槛；对骗子才是。这就是全部设计目标：让诚实者便宜，让造假者昂贵，并且让愿意看的人一眼看得见。
