JEBI TRUST · Data engine数据底座

Every price in this chain begins with a reading taken inside a pod. 这条链上的每一个价格，都始于种植舱里的一次读数。

In conventional coffee the first record is written after harvest, by an interested party, from memory. An aeroponic pod cannot be operated without producing data: the environment is machine-mediated, so roughly 9.3 million readings per cycle arrive as exhaust rather than as a report. Join them to an independent cup score and you hold the only primary dataset in coffee that nobody had to be asked for. 传统咖啡的第一条记录，是在采收之后、由利益相关方、凭记忆写下的。雾化种植舱无法在“不产生数据”的状态下运行：环境由机器中介，所以每个周期约 930 万次读数属于运行废气，而不是一份上报材料。再把它与独立杯测分对接，你就握有咖啡行业唯一一份“不需要征求任何人同意”就能得到的原始数据集。

01 / The instrument采集层

The pod measures before anyone has a reason to lie种植舱在人产生撒谎动机之前，就已经测完了

A farm reports weight and grade at the end of a season, to a buyer it wants to impress. A pod reports temperature, VPD, EC, dissolved oxygen and line pressure every ten seconds, whether or not anyone wants to know. Six channel groups, one identity, one clock. 农场在季末向它想讨好的买方上报重量与等级。种植舱则每十秒上报温度、VPD、EC、溶氧与管路压力——无论有没有人想看。六组通道、一个身份、一只时钟。

The cost difference is structural, not technical. Conventional MRV is expensive because it is a reconstruction: someone must go back and rebuild what happened. Pod data is cheap because it was never a report — it is the control loop's own exhaust. Controlling the environment and recording it are the same act, so the second one is free. 成本差别是结构性的，不是技术性的。传统 MRV 之所以贵，是因为它是“事后重建”：必须有人回头把发生过的事重新拼一遍。舱数据的便宜，来自它从来不是一份报告——它是控制回路自己的废气。控制环境与记录环境是同一个动作，所以后者免费。

02 / Conversion转换

Telemetry is weather. Evidence is a reading attached to an outcome.遥测只是天气。证据是读数接上了结果。

Most data businesses fail at this seam: they sell access to a stream and call it proof. Four rules decide whether a sensor reading may be used to price anything, and they are deliberately boring — boring is what an auditor can repeat. 多数数据生意死在这道缝上：卖的是数据流，却把它叫作证据。以下四条规则决定一条读数是否有资格被用来定价。它们刻意做得很无趣——无趣，才是审计师能重复的东西。

R1
Calibrated已校准Every channel carries a calibration certificate with an expiry date. An out-of-calibration channel is excluded from the evidence set, never interpolated to fill the gap.每个通道都带校准证书与到期日。超出校准期的通道从证据集中剔除，绝不用插值把缺口补上。
R2
Paired已配对A reading becomes evidence only when joined to an independent outcome: cup score, weighed yield, lab assay. Unpaired telemetry is a log file.读数只有在接上独立结果后才成为证据：杯测分、称重产量、化验。未配对的遥测只是一个日志文件。
R3
Complete已完整A failed batch is recorded at the same resolution as a good one. A deletion is itself an event, and it is published as one.失败批次与成功批次按同等粒度记录。删除本身也是一条事件，并且要作为事件披露。
R4
Versioned已版本化Signed at the edge, hashed, daily digest notarised. A correction is a new record that cites the old one — never an overwrite.边缘签名、哈希、每日摘要公证。更正是引用旧记录的新记录——永远不是覆盖。

Five grades, one direction五个等级，只能向上

A token never upgrades the evidence beneath it. A gap at grade 3 is fatal at grade 5, and no amount of legal wrapper hides it. 通证不会让它下面的证据升级。第 3 级的缺口在第 5 级是致命的，再厚的法律外壳也盖不住。

01 / SIGNAL
Signal信号
A raw reading. True if the sensor is online. Consumed by operations.原始读数。传感器在线即为真。使用者：运营。
02 / RECORD
Record记录
Batch-scoped series plus operator sign-off. True if identity is bound.批次范围内的序列 + 操作员签字。身份绑定即为真。
03 / EVIDENCE
Evidence证据
Paired with an independent outcome, calibration current. Consumed by lender and verifier.接上独立结果、校准在有效期。使用者：放款人与核查方。
04 / ASSET
Asset资产
Exclusive rights, valuation, insurance, custody. All nine checks satisfied.独占权利、估值、保险、托管。九项检验全部满足。
05 / TOKEN
Token通证
A claim on the asset, permitted by a written legal opinion. Last, never first.对资产的索取权，须有书面法律意见允许。永远在最后，不在最前。
03 / Architecture结构

Skip a layer and the layer above becomes a story抽掉任何一层，它上面那层就变成故事

Read downward: each layer consumes the one above it. Read upward: each layer returns a fact that makes the one above it cheaper to produce. That two-way traffic is the concept — not the coffee, and not the token. 向下读：每一层都在消耗它上面那一层。向上读：每一层都交回一个事实，使上面那一层更便宜地再生产一次。这条双向通路才是概念本身——不是咖啡，也不是通证。

L0
POD
Primary layer — four primitives原始层——四个基本事实
Whose it is, how much, how good, what it cost the planet. Generated by the machine while it grows the crop; the cost of measurement is already inside the cost of production. 是谁的、有多少、有多好、给地球留下多少成本。由机器在种植过程中生成；计量成本已经包含在生产成本里。
Skip it若抽掉Every layer above degrades into questionnaires and recollection — claims without evidence, printed on nicer paper.上面每一层都退化成问卷与回忆。只有说法、没有证据，只是印刷更精美。
L1
EVIDENCE
Three codes, nine checks三码、九检
Batch code binds lineage, asset code binds ownership, token code binds the claim. Nine binary checks decide whether the evidence may be shown to anyone outside. 批次码绑定血缘，资产码绑定权属，通证码绑定索取权。九项“有/无”检验决定这份证据能否给外部看。
Skip it若抽掉Assets become narratives and the fraud surface is the entire balance sheet.资产变成叙事，欺诈面等于整张资产负债表。
L2
SECTORS
Eight converters八台转换器
Franchise, roasting, carbon, receipt, marketplace, equipment, REIT, AI. Each turns the same four facts into a different product: goods, credit, labels, capacity, recipes, venues. 加盟、烘焙、碳、仓单、商城、设备、REITs、AI。每一台都把同样四个事实变成不同产品：货、信用、标签、产能、配方、场所。
Skip it若抽掉No cash flow. You own a database with no revenue attached to it.没有现金流。你拥有一个数据库，但没有任何收入挂在上头。
L3
CAPITAL
Six asset classes六类可定价资产
Receipt, carbon removal, equipment, IP royalty, store cash flow, REIT units — sequenced by verifiability per unit of legal risk, not by size or story. 仓单、碳移除、设备、IP 版税、门店现金流、REITs 份额——按“每单位法律风险对应的可验证性”排序，不按规模或故事排序。
Skip it若抽掉No exit, no recycling. Expansion is limited to your own cash flow, which is the slowest possible way to build this.没有退出，没有循环。扩张只能靠自有现金流——这是造这件事最慢的方式。

We operate L0 and L1 directly. L2 and L3 are deliberately someone else's balance sheet: the moment the verifier also owns the asset, none of the evidence above is worth anything. 我们直接经营 L0 与 L1。L2 与 L3 刻意放在别人的资产负债表上：一旦核查方同时拥有资产，上面所有证据就一文不值。

06 / Compounding复利

Four loops, and the way each one dies四个回路，以及每一个的死法

A flywheel is a promise. A kill condition is a test. Both are below, and the kill conditions are the ones on the operating dashboard. 飞轮是一句承诺，熔断条件是一次检验。两者都在下面——而挂在运营看板上的是后者。

LOOP L1 / CAPACITY

Cheaper admission, more pods, better model准入更便宜，舱更多，模型更准

pod data舱数据→ franchise underwriting加盟承保→ lower deposit & finance cost押金与融资成本下降→ more pods, more setpoints更多舱、更多设定点→ better forecast预测更准
Kill: pod count grows faster than paired outcomes. Expansion outrunning evidence is the most common way this company would die politely. 熔断：舱数增长快于配对结果的增长。扩张跑在证据前面，是这类公司最常见的“体面死法”。
LOOP L2 / QUALITY

Standardised input makes the curve transferable标准化投入，让曲线可以被转让

controlled input受控投入→ curve as an asset曲线成为资产→ higher, tighter cup score杯测更高更集中→ price premium价格溢价→ asset value资产估值上升
Kill: cup-score variance widens as pods scale. That means the standardisation was in the brochure, not in the setpoints. 熔断：规模扩大后杯测离散度反而变大。这说明标准化写在宣传册里，没写在设定点里。
LOOP L3 / COMPLIANCE

Measured carbon buys market access实测碳，换来市场准入

measured inputs实测投入→ carbon label碳标签→ export access & premium出口准入与溢价→ volume货量→ more batches, better factor更多批次、更准因子
Kill: the methodology is rejected, or a double-counting finding lands. A label that cannot survive a challenge is worse than no label. 熔断：方法学被否，或出现重复计算认定。经不起质疑的标签，比没有标签更糟。
LOOP L4 / CAPITAL

Verified collateral lowers the cost of everything可核验的抵押品，降低一切成本

forward receipt前瞻仓单→ lower haircut折价率下降→ cheaper capital资本成本下降→ capacity产能→ more data更多数据
Kill: the haircut falls faster than data coverage rises. This is the only loop that can turn without new evidence — a lender can always shave a notch to win a deal. 熔断：折价率下降快于数据覆盖上升。这是唯一一个“不需要新证据也能转”的回路——放款人永远可以为了让单降一档。

The dangerous loop is L4, and it looks like success. Every other loop needs a new fact to turn. L4 only needs a competitor. That is why the haircut is capped by data coverage as a matter of policy, not by what the market is willing to lend — leverage that outruns evidence is how this concept dies with a good quarter on the books. 危险的是 L4，而它看起来像成功。其他三个回路都需要新事实才能转，L4 只需要一个竞争对手。所以折价率在制度上被封顶于“数据覆盖度允许的水平”，而不是市场愿意放的水平——杠杆跑在证据前面，就是这个概念在财报最好看的那一季死掉的方式。

07 / The asset资产属性

Collected once, billed eight times, impossible to backfill采集一次、计费八次、无法补录

The collection cost is paid by the first use — usually the operator's own quality control. Every use after that is a new billing event against an asset that already exists. This is the only part of the model where margin improves with time rather than with volume. 采集成本由第一次使用付掉——通常就是运营方自己的品控。此后的每一次使用，都是对一件已存在资产的新计费事件。这是整个模型里唯一一处“利润率随时间改善而不是随规模改善”的地方。

Why it cannot be bought later为什么这份数据以后买不到

A competitor with more money can buy the same sensors, the same pods and the same software. There are five things money does not shorten. 比你更有钱的对手，可以买同样的传感器、同样的舱、同样的软件。但有五样东西，钱买不到时间上的捷径。

M1
Time时间Three years of paired cycles cannot be purchased. Money buys sensors; it does not buy 2026.三年的配对周期买不到。钱能买传感器，买不到 2026 年。
M2
Variance方差One setpoint produces a log; the differences between setpoints produce a response surface. Hence reference pods, deliberately run below optimum at a known cost in yield.单一设定点只产生日志；设定点之间的差异才产生响应曲面。所以我们保留参考舱，故意让它们跑在最优值之外，用可计量的产量换可学习的梯度。
M3
The join连接One lot carries a yield label, a cup label, a carbon label and a price label. No single sector can build that join alone — which is the argument for owning the seam rather than the sector.同一批次同时带着产量标签、杯测标签、碳标签与价格标签。没有任何单一板块能独立建立这个连接——这就是“拥有接缝，而不是拥有板块”的论证。
M4
Negative data负样本Dead batches, low-scoring lots and equipment faults are kept at full resolution. Almost nobody in this industry keeps them, which is exactly why they are the most valuable rows in the table.死批次、低分批次、设备故障，按完整粒度保留。这个行业几乎没人保留这些——所以它们才是表里最贵的行。
M5
Custody监管链Edge signing and a notarised daily digest mean a retroactive edit leaves a trace. Not because a chain is magic, but because the alternative is a spreadsheet someone can quietly fix.边缘签名与每日摘要公证，使“事后修改”必然留下痕迹。不是因为链有什么魔力，而是因为替代方案是一张可以被人悄悄改掉的表格。

Ownership, stated plainly. The grower owns the raw stream. The platform holds an exclusive licence to use it for models and benchmarks. Model weights and derived indices belong to the platform. Aggregates are published. Operator identity is anonymised in-country, and nothing containing personal data crosses a border outside the applicable PIPL process. 数据权属，说清楚。原始数据归种植方所有；平台取得将其用于模型与基准的独占许可；模型权重与衍生指标归平台；聚合结果公开发布；操作员身份在境内匿名化，任何含个人信息的记录都不在适用 PIPL 流程之外跨境。

08 / Falsifiers证伪

Five numbers we would rather publish than discover五个数字，宁可自己公布，也不要被别人发现

An investor should be able to test this thesis without visiting a farm. Each threshold is measured every cycle; two consecutive breaches pause expansion, and the pause is disclosed. 投资者应该不用下地看农场，也能检验这套论点。每个阈值按周期测量；连续两次越界即暂停扩张，且暂停本身要披露。

K1
Cup dispersion杯测离散σ of cup score across pods at identical setpoints > 1.5 points for three cycles. Then "controlled input = controlled cup" is false, and a pod is just a greenhouse.相同设定点下，跨舱杯测标准差连续三个周期 > 1.5 分。那么“受控投入＝受控杯测”不成立，舱体退化为温室。
K2
Calibration drift校准漂移More than 5% drift per channel per quarter, or more than two recalibrations per quarter. Then the cost of evidence exceeds the fee it can support.单通道季度漂移 > 5%，或每季重校超过两次。此时证据成本已高于它能支撑的费用。
K3
Conversion and cycle转化率与周期Cherry-to-green below 55%, or a cycle longer than 120 days. Then the unit economics cannot carry the capital stack above them.樱桃到生豆转化率低于 55%，或单周期超过 120 天。此时单位经济撑不起上面的资本结构。
K4
Pairing coverage配对覆盖Fewer than 30% of batches joined to an independent cup score. Then the dataset is telemetry, not evidence, and no pricing may reference it.能与独立杯测对接的批次低于 30%。此时数据集只是遥测而非证据，任何定价都不得引用它。
K5
Leverage ahead of evidence杠杆跑在证据前Haircut falling faster than coverage rising. Loop L4 is then self-reinforcing, and new credit is suspended by policy — not by the next bad quarter.折价率下降快于覆盖率上升。此时 L4 进入自我强化，按制度暂停新增授信——不等下一个糟糕季度来叫停。
09 / Investor read投资者视角

Same company, three ways to underwrite it同一家公司，三种承保方式

Nobody should have to believe all four layers at once. Each lens below has its own evidence, its own metric and its own failure mode — and they are deliberately not correlated. 没有人需要一次性相信全部四层。下面每一种视角都有自己的证据、自己的指标、自己的失效方式——而且它们刻意互不相关。

LENS 01

Infrastructure yield基础设施收益

You underwrite the pod and the farm as a producing asset, not the data. Demand is existing coffee demand; the output is physical. 你把舱体与农场当作生产性资产来承保，而不是数据。需求是既有的咖啡需求，产出是实物。

Watch:看： capex per tonne, utilisation, electricity price, cycle length.吨产能资本开支、利用率、电价、周期长度。

Killed by:死于： cost per kilo above landed conventional price.每公斤成本高于传统产地到岸价。

LENS 02

Data compounding数据复利

You underwrite the growth of the paired corpus and the fall in forecast error. This is the only lens that improves while nothing is sold. 你承保的是配对语料的增长与预测误差的下降。这是唯一一个“什么都不卖也在变好”的视角。

Watch:看： pairing coverage, share of pods run as reference pods, quarterly model error.配对覆盖率、参考舱占比、模型误差的季度变化。

Killed by:死于： sample volume rising while variance stays flat.样本量上升而方差不变。

LENS 03

Verification toll核查过路费

You underwrite the count of verified events and the number of times each is reused. Metered, contracted, and independent of both the harvest and the token market. 你承保的是已验证事件的数量，以及每一件被复用的次数。按量计量、以合同锁定，与收成和通证市场都不相关。

Watch:看： VE per site per month, reuse per dataset, retention after year one.每站点每月 VE 数、每数据集复用次数、第二年留存。

Killed by:死于： buyers treating verification as a one-off project instead of a subscription.买方把核查当成一次性项目，而不是订阅。

A pod is a machine that grows coffee and, as a by-product, produces the only kind of evidence a lender or an investor can price. The coffee pays for the evidence; the evidence lowers the cost of the coffee. That loop is the whole arbitrage. 种植舱是一台生产咖啡的机器，同时以副产品的形式，产出放款人与投资者唯一能定价的那类证据。咖啡为证据买单，证据又降低咖啡的成本。这个回路，就是全部的套利。
