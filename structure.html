JEBI Trust / Company structure公司结构
02 / Structure结构

The structure is the trust product结构本身，才是信任产品

An investor does not audit a promise. They audit a box: which entity holds the asset, which entity holds the cash, who can move it, and what happens if the group dies. So the box is designed first, and the marketing is written afterwards. 投资者不会审计一句承诺，他们审计的是一个“盒子”：哪个主体持有资产，哪个主体持有现金，谁能动这笔钱，以及集团死掉之后会发生什么。所以先设计盒子，再写宣传。

2.1 Design rules设计规则

Five separations that cannot be traded away五重不可交易的隔离

These are constitutional. Changing any one of them requires a shareholder resolution and a full re-test of every listed asset — which is exactly the cost that keeps them in place. 这五条属于“宪法级”。改动任何一条，都需要股东决议并对全部已挂牌资产重新评级——而这个代价本身就是它们存在的保障。

S1

Issuer ≠ custodian发行方 ≠ 托管方

The entity that benefits from a listing never holds the underlying asset. Custody sits with a licensed third party, contracted to the SPV, not to the group. 从挂牌中获益的主体，绝不持有底层资产。托管由持牌第三方承担，合同签给 SPV，不签给集团。

S2

Issuer ≠ verifier发行方 ≠ 核查方

Sign-off on the nine checks is produced by JT Verification, a ring-fenced company with one revenue line and no distribution rights. It cannot be instructed by an arranger. 九项检验的签字由 JT Verification 出具，该主体只有一条收入线、没有分销权，也不受承销方指令。

S3

Asset ≠ group cash资产 ≠ 集团现金

Every vehicle has its own bank account, its own books, its own auditor sign-off. No cash pooling, no cross-collateral, no cross-default. 每个载体独立银行账户、独立账簿、独立审计签字。不做资金池、不做交叉担保、不设交叉违约。

S4

Technology holds nothing技术主体不持有资产

JEBI Technology runs sensors, ledgers and APIs under a services agreement. It never takes title, never holds keys to client funds, never signs a disclosure. JEBI Technology 依服务协议运行传感器、账本与 API。不取得所有权、不掌握客户资金私钥、不签署披露文件。

S5

Foundation ≠ shareholder基金会 ≠ 股东

The foundation owns the standard — code schema, verification standard, registry rules — and takes no dividend. It can veto a methodology change that weakens evidence. 基金会拥有标准——编码规范、核查标准、登记规则——但不分红。它可否决任何削弱证据要求的方法学修改。

WHY

Cost of a broken box盒子破裂的代价

One commingled account turns every listed asset into an unsecured claim on the group. The whole portfolio re-prices at once. Separation is cheaper than the discount. 一个混同账户，就会把所有已挂牌资产变成对集团的无担保债权，整个组合同时重新定价。隔离的成本，永远低于折价。

2.5 Trust Charter信任宪章

Nine articles, filed with the constitution九条，随公司章程一并归档

A charter that lives in a slide deck is decoration. These sit in the articles of association, in the custody agreement, and in the verification entity's own objects clause — three places that are expensive to change. 写在幻灯片里的宪章只是装饰。以下条款写入公司章程、托管协议、以及评级主体自身的经营范围条款——三个改动成本极高的地方。

A1
Evidence over narrative证据优先于叙事No claim may be published that is not addressable by a code and backed by an evidence hash.任何无法用编码定位、无证据哈希支撑的说法，不得对外发布。
A2
Admission is not negotiable准入不可谈判Fail any one of the nine checks and there is no listing, no investor deck, no exception for revenue, relationship or timing.九项检验中任何一项失败，就不挂牌、不出投资者材料，不因收入、关系或时间节点开例外。
A3
No return is ever promised永不承诺回报No projection is presented as an outcome. Every forward figure carries the assumptions that produce it and the condition that kills it.不把预测当结果呈现。每个前瞻数字必须附带其假设，以及使该假设失效的条件。
A4
One asset, one code, one custodian, one account一资产、一编码、一托管、一账户No asset may appear in two vehicles, two ledgers or two custody statements.同一资产不得出现在两个载体、两本账或两份托管对账单中。
A5
Double pledge is blocked at the system, not discouraged by the contract重复质押由系统阻断，而非靠合同劝阻A pledge lock is written to the registry before financing is released. Release requires the lender's signed confirmation, not the borrower's request.放款前先在登记处写入质押锁定。解押需放款方签署确认，不以借款方申请为准。
A6
24-hour incident disclosure24 小时事件披露Facts, affected codes, containment, remediation, next update time. Silence is treated as a disclosure failure, not as prudence.事实、受影响编码、控制措施、补救方案、下次更新时间。沉默被视为披露失职，而不是谨慎。
A7
Downgrades are published within one business day降级须在一个工作日内公布A missed disclosure triggers an automatic score freeze, then a review. The issuer cannot delay the announcement.披露逾期即触发评分冻结并进入复核。发行方无权延迟公告。
A8
The sign-off cannot be bought签字不可购买Fixed published fee schedule. No performance fee, no success fee, no advisory work for any asset under review.公开固定费率。不对受审资产提供业绩费、成功费或顾问服务。
A9
Wind-down is designed at launch清算方案在发行时确定Every vehicle has a pre-agreed disposal plan, a named liquidator path, and a priority waterfall published before subscription.每个载体在认购前即公布处置预案、清算人路径与清偿顺序。
2.6 Governance治理

Four committees, one external brake四个委员会，一个外部刹车

The group board has five seats, two independent. Each SPV has at least one director who is not employed by the group and whose removal requires trustee consent. 集团董事会五席，其中两席独立。每个 SPV 至少一名非集团雇员的董事，其罢免须经受托人同意。

The Verification Standards Council sits outside the group: five seats, at most two held by JEBI. It can veto any change that lowers an evidence requirement — even if that change would make more assets listable and the company more money. 核查标准委员会设在集团之外：五席，JEBI 最多占两席。它可否决任何降低证据要求的修改——即使该修改能让更多资产上链、让公司赚更多钱。

2.7 Contracts合同

Intercompany relationships are the usual hiding place关联交易，通常是最容易藏东西的地方

Four written agreements, all arm's length四份书面协议，全部按公允原则

  • IP licence — Foundation → Holdings → operating companies. Royalty capped, published.IP 许可——基金会 → 控股 → 运营公司。许可费设上限并公开。
  • Technology services — JEBI Technology → each SPV. Per-call metering, no revenue share.技术服务——技术主体 → 各 SPV。按调用计量，不分收入。
  • Verification services — JT Verification → asset owner. Fixed fee, no volume discount tied to listing count.核查服务——核查主体 → 资产方。固定费，不按挂牌数量给折扣。
  • Administration — HK entity → each SPV. Cost-plus, capped, disclosed in the SPV's accounts.管理服务——香港主体 → 各 SPV。成本加成、设上限，并在 SPV 报表中披露。

What is deliberately absent刻意不设的条款

  • No cross-default between SPVsSPV 之间无交叉违约
  • No group guarantee of any vehicle's obligations集团不为任何载体义务提供担保
  • No cash pooling or intra-group lending to the holdco无资金池，不得向控股层拆借
  • No holdco right to direct a verification outcome控股层无权指示核查结果
  • No revenue-sharing with distributors that scales with investor volume不与分销方按投资者规模分成

Deliberate absence is cheaper to prove than good behaviour.刻意留白，比“表现良好”更容易证明。
