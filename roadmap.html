05 / Roadmap路线图

Four gates, each with a reason to stop四道闸门，每道都有“停下来”的理由

A roadmap without kill criteria is a wish list. Every gate below states what must be true to pass, and what evidence forces the programme to halt — written before the pressure to keep going arrives. 没有终止条件的路线图，只是一张愿望清单。每道闸门都写明“通过必须为真”的条件，以及“必须叫停”的证据——这些都写在“继续往前”的压力到来之前。

G0 · Days 0–90第 0–90 天

Buy the legal answer before buying the software先买法律答案，再买软件

Nothing is built until six written opinions exist. The most common way an RWA programme dies is not a hack — it is discovering in month fourteen that the asset was a security all along. 在六份书面意见齐备之前，什么都不建。RWA 项目最常见的死法不是被黑，而是在第十四个月发现“这项资产从头就是证券”。

Stop rule: if counsel characterises a class as a security and no exemption is available, that class leaves the roadmap for this phase. The roadmap loses a row; the company does not lose its licence. 停止规则：若律师认定某类别构成证券且无可用豁免，该类别在本阶段移出路线图。路线图少一行，公司不会少一张牌照。

  • Holdco + HK entity + foundation incorporated; SPV template executed控股 + 香港主体 + 基金会完成设立；SPV 模板签署
  • Verification standard v1 published: nine checks, with a written evidence definition for each核查标准 v1 公开：九项检验，每项附书面证据定义
  • Registry live with batch codes and asset codes; token codes disabled登记处上线批次码与资产码；通证码关闭
  • Every unverifiable marketing claim removed from existing sites现有网站中所有不可验证的宣传表述已删除

G1 · Months 3–6第 3–6 个月

One receipt, one bank, one red team一张仓单、一家银行、一次红队测试

The pilot is deliberately small: fifty lots, one warehouse, one bank. The objective is not volume — it is to survive an attempt to pledge the same lot twice. 试点刻意做小：五十批、一个仓库、一家银行。目标不是规模，而是撑过一次“把同一批货质押两次”的攻击。

Stop rule: no credit committee approval within two quarters means the receipt thesis is wrong in this market. Stop and re-scope rather than adding marketing. 停止规则：两个季度内没有任何信审通过，说明仓单逻辑在这个市场不成立。停下来重新界定范围，而不是加大宣传。

Exit evidence通过证据

  • ≥ 50 lots registered, each with independent quality certificate and insurance≥ 50 批完成登记，每批具备独立质检与保险
  • Double-pledge block red-teamed: attempt logged, blocked, and reported重复质押拦截完成红队测试：攻击被记录、阻断并报告
  • First nine-check admission records published, pass or fail, with the failing item named首批九检准入记录公布——通过与不通过都公布，并写明未通过项
  • Financing priced and disbursed end-to-end at least once完成至少一次端到端融资定价与放款
G2 · Months 6–9第 6–9 个月

Prove the non-physical assets behave like assets证明“非物理资产”真的像资产

Carbon tests the measurement chain. Equipment tests title perfection. IP tests whether a royalty account can be audited without the licensee's goodwill. 碳检验计量链；设备检验产权完善；IP 检验版税账户能否在不依赖被许可方善意的情况下被审计。

Hard stop: a single confirmed double-count of carbon tonnes ends the carbon line for the year — regardless of revenue, partner pressure, or how small the error was. 硬性停止：出现一次经确认的碳吨重复计算，碳线当年即终止——不论收入、伙伴压力或错误多小。

G3–G4 · Months 9–24第 9–24 个月

The licensed end of the ladder阶梯的持牌末端

5.1 Falsifiable assumptions可证伪的假设

Five bets, each with a test that can fail五个赌注，每个都有会失败的检验

If a test fails twice, the business model changes — not the test. 同一项检验连续失败两次，就改商业模式，不改检验标准。

5.2 What kills it什么会杀死它

Five failure modes, named in advance五种失败模式，事先点名

Naming them is not risk management. Assigning a detector and a trigger to each one is. 把它们说出来不算风控。给每一种配上探测器和触发条件才算。

Indicative first-year budget allocation: legal & compliance 30% · engineering & registry 30% · custody, insurance & audit 20% · commercial 15% · contingency 5%. Compliance is a cost of goods here, not overhead. 首年预算示意分配：法律与合规 30%、工程与登记 30%、托管保险与审计 20%、商务 15%、预备金 5%。在这里，合规是销货成本，不是管理费用。

01
Reclassification重新定性A regulator decides the instrument is a security after launch. Detector: counsel review before each new asset class. Trigger: any unpublished opinion gap.上线后监管认定该工具为证券。探测器：每个新类别上线前的律师复核。触发：任何未公开的意见缺口。
02
Bank withdrawal银行退出The pledgee walks, and the financing chain dies overnight. Detector: concentration of banking partners. Trigger: single-partner dependence above 70%.质权人退出，融资链一夜断裂。探测器：银行伙伴集中度。触发：单一伙伴依赖超过 70%。
03
Data integrity failure数据完整性失效A sensor is spoofed, or a manual entry overrides telemetry. Detector: anomaly rate per batch. Trigger: unexplained override above 2%.传感器被伪造，或人工录入覆盖遥测。探测器：每批次异常率。触发：无法解释的人工覆盖超过 2%。
04
Compliance cost > fee revenue合规成本 > 费用收入The honest version of the model is unaffordable at pilot scale. Detector: cost per listed asset. Trigger: above 40% of that asset's annual fees for two quarters.诚实版本的模式在试点规模下养不起。探测器：单位挂牌资产成本。触发：连续两季超过该资产年费的 40%。
05
Founder dependence创始人依赖Every relationship, opinion and exception routes through one person. Detector: approval counts per signatory. Trigger: a single signatory above 60% of exceptions.所有关系、意见与例外都经过同一个人。探测器：各签署人审批计数。触发：单一签署人处理超过 60% 的例外。

The sequence is the strategy. Receipts before carbon, carbon before equipment, equipment before IP, IP before cash flow, cash flow before REITs — because each step produces the evidence that makes the next step underwritable. Skip one, and the rest is decoration. 顺序就是战略。仓单先于碳，碳先于设备，设备先于 IP，IP 先于现金流，现金流先于 REITs——因为每一步都在生产让下一步可被承保的证据。跳过一步，其余都成了装饰。
